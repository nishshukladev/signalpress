## Papers worth a skim

- **[PAST-TIDE: Prototype-Anchored Statement Tuning with Topic-Invariant Normalization for Stance Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04690)** `[deep,high_value]`
  PAST-TIDE reframes stance detection as masked language modeling with a verbalizer, avoiding randomly initialized heads. It pairs this with prototype-contrastive learning for more stable embeddings in few-shot settings. Verbalizer-based tuning and prototype contrastive loss are reusable patterns for building eval classifiers on scarce labeled data.
